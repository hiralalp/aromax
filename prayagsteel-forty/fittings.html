Buttweld Pipe Fittings

AROMAX OVERSEAS supplies a complete range of buttweld pipe fittings manufactured from premium quality raw materials. Our fittings are designed for seamless connection in piping systems and are available in various materials, sizes, and wall thicknesses.

All fittings conform to ASTM, ASME, MSS-SP standards with complete traceability and test certificates.
