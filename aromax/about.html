Our Story

AROMAX OVERSEAS is a premium manufacturer of custom pipes, flanges, and fasteners, serving industries such as construction, aerospace, oil & gas, petrochemical, and power generation. With our head office in Mumbai, a factory plant in Raigad, and offices in Coimbatore, Uttarakhand, and Hyderabad, we deliver world-class products across India and internationally.

Our journey has been defined by our commitment to precision manufacturing, quality assurance, and building long-lasting relationships with our clients across various industries.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and preferred steel supplier in India, recognized for our quality products, competitive pricing, and exceptional customer service.

We aim to build lasting partnerships with our customers by consistently delivering value and exceeding expectations.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality steel products at competitive prices while maintaining the highest standards of integrity and customer service.
