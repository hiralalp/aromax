Industrial Pipes & Tubes

AROMAX OVERSEAS offers a comprehensive range of seamless and welded pipes & tubes in various materials, sizes, and specifications. Our pipes are used in critical applications including oil & gas, petrochemical, power plants, refineries, and process industries worldwide.

We supply pipes conforming to ASTM, ASME, DIN, EN, JIS standards with complete mill test certificates and third-party inspection reports.

Stainless Steel Pipes

Premium SS pipes in grades 304, 304L, 316, 316L, 321, 347. Available in seamless and welded types with polished or mill finish.

  • Seamless Pipes (ASTM A312)
  • Welded Pipes (ASTM A358)
  • ERW Pipes
  • EFW Pipes
