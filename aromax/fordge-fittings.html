Forged Pipe Fittings

AROMAX OVERSEAS offers premium quality forged fittings for high-pressure piping systems. Our forged fittings are manufactured from solid bar stock and are available in socket weld and threaded configurations.

Available in pressure classes 2000#, 3000#, 6000#, and 9000# conforming to ASME B16.11 standards.
