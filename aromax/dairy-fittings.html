Sanitary Fittings

AROMAX OVERSEAS offers a complete range of dairy and sanitary fittings for food processing, pharmaceutical, beverage, and cosmetic industries. Our fittings are manufactured from food-grade stainless steel with high polish finish for easy cleaning and sanitization.
